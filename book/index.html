After a 5 star review from The Obscuity and a 4 star review from London Pub Theatres, 1816 is trying to make the journey to the Edinburgh Fringe 2026! We are an independent company formed of alumni from the University of Cambridge. The show has been extremely well received in London and Cambridge and we would like to share it even further. Anything and everything is appreciated; venue fees are extremely expensive at the fringe and we are working from very little funds. Help our lovely little show reach Edinburgh!
